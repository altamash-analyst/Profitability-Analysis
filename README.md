# Profitability Analysis - Power BI Dashboard

## Business Intelligence | Data Analytics | Power BI

A complete **Profitability Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze revenue, costs, profitability, margins, products, categories, brands, customers, regions, sales channels, discounts, returns and other business performance factors.

This project demonstrates the practical application of **Power BI, Power Query, DAX and Excel** to transform business data into an interactive and decision-support dashboard.

---

## Project Overview

The **Profitability Analysis** project is a Business Intelligence dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive view of business profitability and its major drivers.

The dashboard converts raw business data into interactive visualizations and KPIs that help analyze:

- Revenue performance
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Gross Profit
- Net Profit
- Gross Margin
- Net Profit Margin
- Product profitability
- Category profitability
- Brand performance
- Customer profitability
- Regional performance
- Sales channel performance
- Discount impact
- Returns
- Shipping costs
- Overall profitability trends

The project focuses on presenting financial and operational information in a clear and interactive format.

# Business Problem

Businesses generate large amounts of sales and operational data, but raw data alone does not provide an effective understanding of profitability.

The key challenge addressed by this project is:

> How can business data be transformed into an interactive dashboard that helps management understand revenue, costs, profit margins and the factors affecting profitability?

The dashboard provides a centralized analytical view to support better understanding of business performance.

---

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze overall business profitability.
2. Monitor revenue and cost performance.
3. Calculate gross and net profitability.
4. Analyze profitability by product.
5. Compare different product categories.
6. Analyze brand-level performance.
7. Understand customer profitability.
8. Examine regional performance.
9. Compare sales channels.
10. Analyze the impact of discounts.
11. Analyze product returns.
12. Understand shipping cost impact.
13. Create an interactive Power BI reporting environment.
14. Present complex business information through simple visualizations.
